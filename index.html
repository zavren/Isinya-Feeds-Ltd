Choose a suitable age for your poultry project. Prices marked indicative are planning guides, not confirmed quotations.

Planning tip: Point-of-lay birds reduce the time spent raising chicks before egg production, while day-old chicks may lower the initial cost per bird. Compare feed, housing, mortality, vaccination and rearing costs before deciding. Commercial layers commonly begin laying around 18–20 weeks, but this varies.

About Isinya Feeds

Supporting Farmers Beyond the Feed Store

At Isinya Feeds Ltd, we are committed to helping farmers access quality poultry products and practical farming solutions.

Located in Kisaju, Kajiado County along the Nairobi-Namanga Road, we serve farmers with poultry products, feeds and farming support designed around their day-to-day needs.

Our approach is simple: provide dependable products, clear communication and practical support that helps farmers make informed decisions.
